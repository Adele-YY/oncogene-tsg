Ras (KRAS/HRAS/NRAS)
Oncogene
Point Mutation (missense) · G12, G13, Q61
Mechanism 机制图

Normal Function 正常功能

Small GTPase (G蛋白): molecular switch transducing signals from RTK → MAPK & PI3K pathways. Cycles between GDP (off) and GTP (on).

Mutation Effect 突变影响

Missense mutations block GTP hydrolysis → Ras trapped in GTP-bound active state → constitutive downstream signaling regardless of growth signals.

Common Cancer 常见癌症

Pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma ~90% (KRAS)

Targeted Therapy 靶向治疗

Sotorasib / Adagrasib (KRAS G12C inhibitors). Historically "undruggable"; G12C covalent inhibitors breakthrough.

Myc (c-Myc)
Oncogene
Amplification / Translocation t(8;14)
Mechanism 机制图

Normal Function 正常功能

Transcription factor (转录因子): master regulator of cell cycle progression, metabolism, ribosome biogenesis, and protein synthesis. Rapidly turned over.

Mutation Effect 突变影响

Gene amplification or translocation (IgH enhancer in Burkitt) → massive overexpression → global transcriptional amplification driving proliferation and biomass accumulation.

Common Cancer 常见癌症

Burkitt lymphoma ~100% (t(8;14) translocation)

Targeted Therapy 靶向治疗

No direct FDA-approved Myc inhibitor. Indirect: BET inhibitors (JQ1, OTX015) block Myc transcription; OmoMYC dominant-negative peptide (clinical trials).

PIK3CA (p110α)
Oncogene
Point Mutation · E542K / E545K / H1047R
Mechanism 机制图

Normal Function 正常功能

Catalytic subunit p110α of PI3K (磷脂酰肌醇3-激酶): phosphorylates PIP2 → PIP3 at membrane, recruiting and activating AKT. Requires upstream RTK/Ras signal.

Mutation Effect 突变影响

Hotspot mutations in helical (E545K) or kinase (H1047R) domain → constitutive lipid kinase activity independent of upstream signals → PIP3 overproduction → AKT pathway hyperactivation.

Common Cancer 常见癌症

Breast cancer (HR+/HER2−) ~40%

Targeted Therapy 靶向治疗

Alpelisib (Piqray) — PI3Kα-specific inhibitor, approved for HR+/HER2− metastatic breast cancer with PIK3CA mutation, combined with fulvestrant.

MDM2
Oncogene
Gene Amplification (12q15)
Mechanism 机制图

Normal Function 正常功能

E3 ubiquitin ligase (E3泛素连接酶): binds p53, ubiquitinates it → proteasomal degradation. Key negative regulator keeping p53 levels low under normal conditions. Negative feedback loop.

Mutation Effect 突变影响

Gene amplification → MDM2 overproduction → excessive p53 ubiquitination and degradation → p53 tumor suppressor pathway functionally inactivated even when TP53 itself is wild-type.

Common Cancer 常见癌症

Well-differentiated / dedifferentiated liposarcoma ~90%

Targeted Therapy 靶向治疗

MDM2-p53 interaction inhibitors: Idasanutlin (RG7388), Milademetan (DS-3032b), Siremadlin. Restore p53 by blocking MDM2 binding. Clinical trials; milademetan approved in some regions.

AKT (AKT1/2/3, PKB)
Oncogene
Point Mutation (AKT1 E17K) / Amplification
Mechanism 机制图

Normal Function 正常功能

Serine/threonine kinase (丝氨酸/苏氨酸激酶): central node downstream of PI3K. Phosphorylates targets (mTOR, GSK3β, BAD, FoxO) to promote survival, growth, glucose uptake, and metabolism.

Mutation Effect 突变影响

AKT1 E17K mutation in PH domain → constitutive plasma membrane recruitment independent of PIP3 → persistent phosphorylation/activation. AKT2/AKT3 amplification → overexpression → pathway hyperactivation.

Common Cancer 常见癌症

Breast cancer (AKT1 E17K) ~3–5%; also ovarian, colorectal

Targeted Therapy 靶向治疗

Capivasertib (Truqap) — AKT inhibitor approved for HR+/HER2− breast cancer with PIK3CA/AKT1/PTEN alteration. Ipatasertib, Afuresertib in trials.

Cyclin D (CCND1/2/3)
Oncogene
Amplification / Translocation t(11;14)
Mechanism 机制图

Normal Function 正常功能

Regulatory subunit (调节亚基): binds and activates CDK4/6. Cyclin D-CDK4/6 complex phosphorylates Rb protein → releases E2F transcription factor → drives G1→S phase transition. Mitogen-responsive.

Mutation Effect 突变影响

CCND1 amplification or t(11;14) translocation (IgH enhancer in mantle cell lymphoma) → Cyclin D1 overexpression → excessive CDK4/6 activation → Rb constitutively phosphorylated → uncontrolled G1/S progression and proliferation.

Common Cancer 常见癌症

Mantle cell lymphoma ~100% (t(11;14))

Targeted Therapy 靶向治疗

CDK4/6 inhibitors (indirectly target Cyclin D pathway): Palbociclib (Ibrance), Ribociclib (Kisqali), Abemaciclib (Verzenio) — standard for HR+/HER2− metastatic breast cancer.

CDK6
Oncogene
Amplification / Overexpression
Mechanism 机制图

Normal Function 正常功能

Serine/threonine kinase (丝氨酸/苏氨酸激酶): partners with Cyclin D to phosphorylate Rb in G1 phase, promoting cell cycle entry. Also has kinase-independent roles in hematopoiesis and immune regulation.

Mutation Effect 突变影响

Gene amplification or transcriptional upregulation → excess CDK6 protein → increased Rb phosphorylation even without sufficient mitogenic signals → accelerated G1/S transition and unchecked proliferation.

Common Cancer 常见癌症

Glioblastoma ~10% (CDK6 amplification); also lymphoma, leukemia

Targeted Therapy 靶向治疗

CDK4/6 inhibitors: Palbociclib, Ribociclib, Abemaciclib. Abemaciclib has higher CDK6 selectivity. Used in HR+/HER2− breast cancer; trials in glioblastoma and lymphoma.

β-catenin (CTNNB1)
Oncogene
Point Mutation (N-terminal, S33/S37/T41/S45)
Mechanism 机制图

Normal Function 正常功能

Dual-function protein (双重功能蛋白): (1) Cell adhesion — links cadherins to actin cytoskeleton; (2) Wnt signaling effector — when stabilized, translocates to nucleus and co-activates TCF/LEF transcription of proliferation genes.

Mutation Effect 突变影响

N-terminal missense mutations destroy GSK3β phosphorylation sites (S33, S37, T41, S45) → β-catenin escapes ubiquitination and proteasomal degradation → accumulates in nucleus → constitutive Wnt target gene transcription independent of Wnt ligand.

Common Cancer 常见癌症

Hepatocellular carcinoma ~20–30%; also desmoid tumors, endometrial

Targeted Therapy 靶向治疗

No FDA-approved direct β-catenin inhibitor. Wnt pathway inhibitors in trials: Porcupine inhibitors (LGK974/WNT974), tankyrase inhibitors, BC2059 (tegatrabetan). Surgery/NSAIDs for desmoid.

ERBB2 (HER2/Neu)
Oncogene
Amplification (HER2+) / Point Mutation
Mechanism 机制图

Normal Function 正常功能

Receptor tyrosine kinase (受体酪氨酸激酶): EGFR/ERBB family member. No known ligand; preferred heterodimerization partner for HER1/3/4. Activates PI3K/AKT and RAS/MAPK pathways to drive proliferation and survival.

Mutation Effect 突变影响

ERBB2 gene amplification → 10–100× more HER2 protein on cell surface → spontaneous homodimerization and constitutive kinase activation without ligand → persistent downstream signaling → aggressive proliferation and survival.

Common Cancer 常见癌症

Breast cancer ~15–20% (HER2+ subtype)

Targeted Therapy 靶向治疗

Trastuzumab (Herceptin), Pertuzumab, T-DM1 (Kadcyla), T-DXd (Enhertu), Lapatinib, Neratinib, Tucatinib, Margetuximab. HER2 is one of the most successfully targeted oncoproteins.

HIF1A (HIF-1α)
Oncogene (stabilized)
Stabilization / Overexpression (rare direct mutation)
Mechanism 机制图

Normal Function 正常功能

Transcription factor α-subunit (转录因子α亚基): master regulator of cellular response to hypoxia (低氧). Heterodimerizes with HIF-1β (ARNT) to activate genes for angiogenesis (VEGF), glycolysis (GLUT1), erythropoiesis (EPO), and survival.

Mutation Effect 突变影响

Rarely directly mutated. Typically stabilized by VHL loss-of-function (see VHL card) or tumor hypoxia. Under normoxia, HIF-1α is hydroxylated and VHL-targeted for degradation. Loss of VHL → HIF-1α constitutively stable → pseudo-hypoxic response → angiogenesis and metabolic reprogramming fueling tumor growth.

Common Cancer 常见癌症

Clear cell renal cell carcinoma (via VHL loss) ~70–90%

Targeted Therapy 靶向治疗

Indirect: Anti-angiogenics (Bevacizumab anti-VEGF, Sunitinib, Pazopanib, Axitinib). HIF-2α-specific inhibitor Belzutifan (Welireg) approved for VHL disease/ccRCC (targets HIF2A paralog, not HIF1A directly).

p21 (CDKN1A / CIP1)
Tumor Suppressor
Deletion / Downregulation / Epigenetic silencing
Mechanism 机制图

Normal Function 正常功能

CDK inhibitor (CDK抑制因子, CIP/KIP family): p53 transcriptional target. Binds and inhibits Cyclin E/A-CDK2 and Cyclin D-CDK4/6 complexes → enforces G1 cell cycle arrest after DNA damage, allowing repair. Also promotes senescence and differentiation.

Mutation Effect 突变影响

Rarely somatically mutated; more often downregulated via deletion, epigenetic silencing, or p53 dysfunction (since p21 is a p53 target). Loss → CDK complexes remain active → defective G1/S checkpoint → cells with damaged DNA proceed to replicate → genomic instability and tumor progression.

Common Cancer 常见癌症

Various cancers with p53 pathway dysfunction; reduced in endometrial, prostate, lung

Targeted Therapy 靶向治疗

No direct p21-targeted drug. Indirect strategies: p53 gene therapy (restores p21 transcription), MDM2 inhibitors (raise p53 → p21), CDK4/6 inhibitors (compensate for lost p21 inhibition). Histone deacetylase inhibitors can re-express p21.

APC
Tumor Suppressor
Truncating Mutation (nonsense/frameshift) · LOH
Mechanism 机制图

Normal Function 正常功能

Scaffold tumor suppressor (支架抑癌蛋白): core component of β-catenin destruction complex (with Axin, GSK3β, CK1α). Binds and phosphorylates β-catenin → ubiquitination → degradation. Also regulates cell migration, adhesion, and chromosome segregation.

Mutation Effect 突变影响

Truncating mutations (nonsense/frameshift) remove β-catenin binding/SAMP repeats → destruction complex cannot assemble → β-catenin escapes degradation → nuclear accumulation → constitutive Wnt target gene transcription (MYC, CCND1) → uncontrolled proliferation of intestinal epithelium → adenoma formation.

Common Cancer 常见癌症

Colorectal cancer ~80% (sporadic); FAP (germline, 100% lifetime risk)

Targeted Therapy 靶向治疗

No direct APC-targeted therapy. Wnt pathway inhibitors in clinical trials (Porcupine inhibitors, tankyrase inhibitors). FAP management: endoscopic surveillance, prophylactic colectomy, NSAIDs (sulindac, celecoxib) reduce polyp burden. Standard CRC chemo.

BRCA1
Tumor Suppressor
Germline/Somatic Loss-of-Function · Deletion
Mechanism 机制图

Normal Function 正常功能

DNA repair protein (DNA修复蛋白): tumor suppressor critical for homologous recombination (HR) repair of DNA double-strand breaks. Recruits RAD51 to break sites, interacts with BRCA2/PALB2, enforces cell cycle checkpoints (G2/M), and maintains genomic stability.

Mutation Effect 突变影响

Loss-of-function mutations (truncating, missense in BRCT/RING domains) → defective HR repair → cells rely on error-prone non-homologous end joining (NHEJ) → accumulation of chromosomal rearrangements, deletions, and mutations → genomic instability ("BRCAness") → malignant transformation.

Common Cancer 常见癌症

Hereditary breast & ovarian cancer (HBOC): breast ~55–65%, ovarian ~39% (germline carriers)

Targeted Therapy 靶向治疗

PARP inhibitors (合成致死 synthetic lethality): Olaparib (Lynparza), Talazoparib (Talzenna), Niraparib (Zejula), Rucaparib (Rubraca). Platinum-based chemotherapy (cisplatin/carboplatin) also effective due to HR deficiency. Preventive surgery for high-risk carriers.

TP53 (p53)
Tumor Suppressor
Missense Mutation (dominant-negative / GOF) · Most mutated gene in cancer
Mechanism 机制图

Normal Function 正常功能

"Guardian of the genome" (基因组守护者): transcription factor and tumor suppressor. Activated by DNA damage, oncogene stress, hypoxia → transcribes p21 (cell cycle arrest), BAX/PUMA (apoptosis), GADD45 (DNA repair), and senescence genes. Eliminates or repairs damaged cells.

Mutation Effect 突变影响

Mostly missense mutations in DNA-binding domain (hotspots R175, G245, R248, R249, R273, R282). Three effects: (1) Loss of tumor suppressor function; (2) Dominant-negative — mutant p53 monomers poison wildtype tetramers; (3) Gain-of-function (GOF) — mutant protein acquires new oncogenic activities promoting metastasis, drug resistance, and metabolism changes.

Common Cancer 常见癌症

~50% of all human cancers. High-grade serous ovarian ~96%; also lung, HNSCC, colorectal. Li-Fraumeni syndrome (germline)

Targeted Therapy 靶向治疗

No universally approved direct p53 drug. APR-246/Eprenetapopt (mutant p53 reactivator, approved in some countries). MDM2 inhibitors (for WT p53 tumors: idasanutlin, siremadlin). p53 vaccine and gene therapy (Gendicine in China) in use/trials. Most cancers rely on chemo/immunotherapy.

PTEN
Tumor Suppressor
Loss-of-Function Mutation · Deletion · Epigenetic silencing
Mechanism 机制图

Normal Function 正常功能

Lipid phosphatase (脂质磷酸酶): major antagonist of PI3K/AKT pathway. Dephosphorylates PIP3 → PIP2 at the plasma membrane, removing the docking site for AKT and PDK1. Keeps AKT activity in check, restraining proliferation and survival. Also has nuclear and protein phosphatase functions.

Mutation Effect 突变影响

Loss-of-function (missense in phosphatase domain, truncating, deletion, promoter methylation) → loss of PIP3 phosphatase activity → PIP3 accumulates at membrane → constitutive AKT recruitment and activation → enhanced mTOR signaling, glucose metabolism, survival, and proliferation. Even partial loss (haploinsufficiency) promotes tumorigenesis.

Common Cancer 常见癌症

Glioblastoma (primary) ~40%; Endometrial ~30–50%; also prostate, breast, thyroid. Cowden syndrome (germline)

Targeted Therapy 靶向治疗

Indirect targeting of downstream PI3K/AKT/mTOR axis: Everolimus/Temsirolimus (mTOR inhibitors, approved for subependymal giant cell astrocytoma in TSC, renal cell carcinoma, breast cancer). Alpelisib (PI3Kα inhibitor). AKT inhibitors (capivasertib). No direct PTEN restoration therapy approved.

VHL (von Hippel-Lindau)
Tumor Suppressor
Germline/Somatic Loss-of-Function · Promoter methylation
Mechanism 机制图

Normal Function 正常功能

Substrate recognition subunit of E3 ubiquitin ligase (E3泛素连接酶底物识别亚基): VCB-CUL2 complex. Under normoxia, HIF-α subunits are prolyl-hydroxylated (oxygen-dependent). VHL recognizes hydroxylated HIF-α → ubiquitinates → proteasomal degradation. Ensures hypoxic response genes are OFF when oxygen is available.

Mutation Effect 突变影响

Loss-of-function (missense in HIF-binding domain, truncating, deletion, promoter hypermethylation) → VHL cannot bind hydroxylated HIF-α → HIF-1α/HIF-2α escape degradation even under normoxia → constitutive "pseudo-hypoxic" transcriptional program → VEGF-driven angiogenesis, glycolytic switch (Warburg), erythropoiesis → highly vascular tumors.

Common Cancer 常见癌症

Clear cell renal cell carcinoma ~90% have VHL inactivation (~70–80% somatic mutation)

Targeted Therapy 靶向治疗

Belzutifan (Welireg) — HIF-2α inhibitor, first-in-class, approved for VHL disease-associated tumors and advanced ccRCC. Anti-angiogenics: Sunitinib, Pazopanib, Axitinib, Bevacizumab (anti-VEGF). mTOR inhibitors: Everolimus, Temsirolimus. Immune checkpoint inhibitors increasingly used.

RUNX1 (AML1 / CBFα2)
Tumor Suppressor / Fusion Oncogene
Translocation t(8;21) · Loss-of-Function Mutation
Mechanism 机制图

Normal Function 正常功能

Master transcription factor (核心转录因子): alpha subunit of Core Binding Factor (CBF), heterodimerizes with CBFβ. Essential for definitive hematopoiesis — regulates differentiation of myeloid and lymphoid lineages by activating genes for myeloid maturation, T-cell development, and megakaryocyte differentiation.

Mutation Effect 突变影响

Two mechanisms: (1) Translocation t(8;21)(q22;q22) → RUNX1-RUNX1T1 (ETO) fusion protein — acts as dominant-negative transcriptional repressor, blocking myeloid differentiation; (2) Loss-of-function point mutations/deletions → impaired hematopoietic differentiation. Both result in accumulation of immature myeloid blasts → leukemogenesis. RUNX1 mutations also associated with therapy-related AML and MDS progression.

Common Cancer 常见癌症

Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML): t(8;21) ~10–15% of AML; RUNX1 mutations ~10–15%

Targeted Therapy 靶向治疗

No direct RUNX1-targeted therapy approved. t(8;21) AML (favorable-risk) treated with standard chemotherapy: Cytarabine + Anthracycline ("7+3"), high-dose cytarabine consolidation. Gemtuzumab ozogamicin (anti-CD33 ADC) added in some regimens. Allogeneic HSCT for high-risk. RUNX1 inhibitors and degraders in early clinical trials.
